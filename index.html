1. Greetings (Ungkapan Sapaan)

Ungkapan yang digunakan saat pertama kali bertemu atau menyapa seseorang.

Greeting (Sapaan) Response (Jawaban) Keterangan Waktu / Situasi
Good morning! Good morning! Pagi hari (00.00 – 12.00)
Good afternoon! Good afternoon! Siang/Sore hari (12.00 – 18.00)
Good evening! Good evening! Malam hari saat bertemu (18.00 – 24.00)
Hello! / Hi! Hello! / Hi! Situasi santai (informal)
How are you? I'm fine, thank you. / I'm good. Menanyakan kabar
How do you do? How do you do? Formal, saat baru pertama kali bertemu

2. Introducing Yourself & Others (Perkenalan)

Ungkapan penting saat memperkenalkan diri kepada teman baru di sekolah:

Fungsi Contoh Kalimat
Memulai perkenalan Let me introduce myself. / Hi, I would like to introduce myself.
Menyebutkan nama My name is Rafi. / I am Rafi. / You can call me Rafi.
Menyebutkan asal I am from Bandung. / I come from Medan.
Menyebutkan tempat tinggal I live on Jalan Merdeka. / I live in Surabaya.
Menyebutkan usia & hobi I am 12 years old. / My hobby is reading books.
Ungkapan senang bertemu Nice to meet you! / Pleased to meet you!
Respon senang bertemu Nice to meet you, too!
Catatan Penting: Gunakan "Nice to meet you" hanya saat pertama kali berkenalan dengan seseorang. Jika sudah pernah bertemu sebelumnya, gunakan "Nice to see you again".

3. Leave-Taking (Ungkapan Berpamitan)

Ungkapan yang diucapkan ketika hendak berpisah atau mengakhiri percakapan.

Leave-Taking (Pamitan) Response (Jawaban)
Goodbye! / Bye! Goodbye! / Bye!
See you later! / See you tomorrow! See you!
Good night! Good night! (Diucapkan saat berpisah malam hari / hendak tidur)
Take care! You too! Thanks.

4. Contoh Percakapan (Dialogue)

Situasi: Siswa baru berkenalan di halaman sekolah

Dina: Good morning! Are you a new student here?

Bima: Good morning! Yes, I am. My name is Bima. What is your name?

Dina: I'm Dina. Where are you from, Bima?

Bima: I am from Yogyakarta. Now I live on Jalan Diponegoro.

Dina: Welcome to our school, Bima! Nice to meet you.

Bima: Thank you, Dina. Nice to meet you, too.

Dina: Oh, the bell is ringing. See you later!

Bima: See you!
